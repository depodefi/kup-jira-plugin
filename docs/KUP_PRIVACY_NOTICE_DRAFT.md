## 1. Scope

This notice explains how the App processes personal data when a customer
installs and uses it in Atlassian Jira Cloud. The App helps customers record
eligible 50% KUP creative-work hours on Jira issues, calculate monthly KUP
percentages, manage approvals, and create payroll summaries.

For App data, the customer organisation normally determines the purposes for
which its users' data is processed. Veloscope processes that data only to
provide, secure, support, and improve the App in accordance with the
customer's instructions and applicable law. The customer is responsible for
informing its Jira users about its use of the App.

## 2. Personal data the App processes

The App processes only the Jira and KUP information needed for its functions.

| Data category | Examples | Why the App uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jira account identifiers | Atlassian account ID of an employee, manager, administrator, approver, or editor | Identifying records, applying manager access, and handling privacy lifecycle requests |
| KUP work records | Reporting month, eligible KUP hours, Jira issue association, approval status | Producing employee and manager monthly reports and calculating KUP percentages |
| Working-time adjustments | Monthly absence hours, overtime hours, employee account ID, and editor account ID | Calculating an employee's effective monthly working-hour baseline |
| Administration and access configuration | Eligible Jira projects and issue types, reporting months, monthly baselines, KUP limits, manager account IDs, and manager-team membership | Configuring the App and limiting manager reporting access |
| Approval and audit information | Approver or acting-user account ID, timestamps, change history, and issue key | Locking approved records, supporting traceability, and resolving payroll or support questions |
| Generated export data | Employee and approver names, relevant KUP and working-time data, and payroll-summary fields | Creating a customer-requested CSV or XLSX payroll summary for download |

The App resolves display names from Jira when it needs to display them. It does
not persist email addresses, Jira passwords, Atlassian API tokens, or user
profile display names in its normal configuration records. A generated payroll
export can contain names obtained from Jira because that is necessary for the
customer's payroll workflow.

## 3. How we use the data

We use the data above to:

- record and show KUP hours associated with Jira issues;
- calculate monthly KUP percentages using customer-configured working-hour
  baselines and adjustments;
- provide administrator and manager access controls, reviews, approvals, and
  approval locks;
- create requested CSV and XLSX payroll summaries;
- maintain an audit trail of KUP and approval changes;
- respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and maintain the App; and
- meet the App's privacy lifecycle obligations, including identifying and
  erasing data related to a closed Atlassian account.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or cross-customer
profiling.

## 4. Where data is stored and who receives it

KUP records and related approval/audit properties are stored on Jira issues.
The App also uses Atlassian Forge storage for configuration, manager teams,
monthly adjustments, approval logs, temporary exports, and its privacy
lifecycle registry. The App does not send App data to a separate Veloscope
server or other third-party analytics, advertising, or payroll service.

Atlassian provides Jira Cloud and the Forge platform that host and process this
data. Their handling of data is governed by the customer's agreement with
Atlassian and applicable Atlassian terms. Data location, residency options, and
international transfers available through Jira Cloud and Forge are governed by
Atlassian's applicable documentation and customer agreement.

We may disclose data only where necessary to provide the App, comply with law,
protect the App or its users, or as instructed or authorised by the customer.

## 5. Access and permissions

The App requests the minimum Jira and Forge permissions required for its
features: reading and writing the relevant Jira work data, reading Jira user
information to resolve and apply account identities, app storage, Jira
configuration access required for the configured manager/group filtering, and
Forge's personal-data reporting capability. The Marketplace Privacy & Security
tab describes the current permissions and data practices for the released
version of the App.

## 6. Retention

We retain App data only for as long as needed to provide the feature for which
it was collected, subject to the customer's actions and any applicable legal
obligations.

| Data | Retention rule |
| --- | --- |
| KUP issue records and approval properties | Retained on the Jira issue until changed, removed, or erased through the privacy lifecycle described below |
| Per-issue audit history | The most recent 50 audit entries per issue are retained; older entries are replaced as new entries are added |
| Monthly adjustments, configuration, and manager-team records | Retained until changed, removed by the customer, or erased through the privacy lifecycle described below |
| Monthly approval log | The most recent 500 entries per reporting month are retained; older entries are replaced as new entries are added |
| Generated payroll export | Deleted after download or automatically after one hour, whichever occurs first |

## 7. Account closure, deletion, and uninstallation

The App uses Forge's personal-data reporting mechanism to report retained
Atlassian account IDs. When Atlassian notifies the App that an account is
closed, the App erases the related KUP issue records, adjustments, manager/team
references, approval/audit references, active temporary exports, and its
privacy registry entry. If an erasure attempt fails, it is retained for a
later retry.

Jira administrators may also request deletion or correction by contacting
[PRIVACY EMAIL ADDRESS] and identifying the Jira site, the relevant account or
records, and the request. We may need to verify the request with the customer
